A letter from Hugo and Anis

Dear Sergii and Alex,

Thank you for the call, and for being so clear about where Minima stands.

Your pilots are promising, and they came through warm introductions and Berkeley SkyDeck. Alex wants to know whether the pitch works with strangers. So do we.

You've built the technology. We test where it wins with buyers who don't know you, then turn what we learn into a sharper buyer story and into materials you can show investors, whatever the numbers say.

We work as forward-deployed partners, not as a tool you log into or an agency that hands over a playbook. We sit inside your team and your weekly rhythm, and we run one loop that learns: signals, decisions, actions and feedback. We deploy each play with you, test it on real buyers and keep what works. Every call you take feeds back in, so the targeting and the message get sharper as we go.

We kept the plan narrow on purpose. We lead with faster responses, not cheaper tokens, following Sergii's point on the call. Self-hosted runs as a smaller second track. No performance number goes out until you clear it. Our system creates the opening, and you run the sales conversations.

The plan, the price and the limits are below. Where something is our proposal rather than something we agreed, we say so.

With care,

Hugo & Anis

P.S. The first thing we ask for is a list of everyone you already know. We keep them out of the outreach, so the replies we report come from people outside your known network.
